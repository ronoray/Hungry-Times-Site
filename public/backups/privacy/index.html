Data handling

Privacy policy

How HungryTimes Backups handles the data needed for private backup, synchronization and recovery.

Effective and last updated: 21 September 2026.

1. Who operates this utility

HungryTimes Backups is operated by Hungry Times for its owner and explicitly authorized operators. It runs on owner-managed computers and servers and uses rclone to work with an authorized Google Drive account. It is not a public backup service. This policy covers this utility and its information pages, rather than the restaurant's ordering service.

Contact: admin@hungrytimes.in.

2. Information accessed and processed

  • Selected files and archives: application databases, configuration, documents and other personal or business files selected by the owner for backup or synchronization. Their contents may include information already held in the source systems.
  • Google Drive metadata: file and folder names and identifiers, sizes, modification times, available checksums and other metadata needed to locate, verify and manage the selected files.
  • Authorization credentials: OAuth client details, access tokens and refresh tokens used to maintain the owner's authorized connection. The utility does not collect the Google account password.
  • Operational records: timestamps, file paths, transfer outcomes, exit codes, durations, error messages and backup status. Logs can contain filenames and other metadata from the selected files.

3. Google permissions and purpose

Google Drive access is used to create backup folders, list files, upload backups, download files for verification or restoration, synchronize selected files and remove older copies under configured retention rules. These operations require read and write access to the authorized Drive account.

A broad Drive authorization can technically permit access to files outside an individual backup folder. The configured workflows use that access for the owner's selected operations; a folder choice is not a separate Google permission boundary. The authorization screen shows the permissions being requested before the owner grants access.

Google user data is used only to provide and maintain these backup, synchronization, verification, monitoring and recovery functions. The utility does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

4. Where information is stored

Backup content is stored in the authorized Google Drive account. Source data, staging files, retained local archives, verification downloads and restore copies may also be stored on the owner's computers or servers. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the configured rclone credential files on those systems and may be included in the owner's private configuration backups.

Operational logs and status records are stored on the systems running the jobs. Google Drive API connections use HTTPS. Local files and archives are subject to the security and encryption settings of their host systems; this utility does not promise that every local copy or backup archive has additional encryption.

5. Service providers and notifications

Google processes authorization requests, API requests and files stored in Google Drive as part of providing those services. The owner's hosting providers may process the data stored on hosted servers.

When enabled, Telegram receives operational messages sent to the owner's private chat. These may include backup filenames, sizes, timestamps, freshness information and error summaries. Backup archives and database contents are not attached by the backup status monitor. The owner can disable these notifications in the monitoring configuration.

Data is not made public by these backup jobs. Access is limited to the owner, explicitly authorized operators and the providers needed to perform the described operations. Any further sharing requires the owner's authorization or an applicable legal requirement.

6. Retention and deletion

Retention varies by backup category and the owner's settings. Scheduled jobs can remove older local or remote copies after newer backups are made. Logs, verification files and separately created restore copies can have different retention periods and may require manual removal.

Deleting a Drive backup may first move it to Google Drive's trash, depending on the configured operation. Permanent removal may require emptying the trash and is subject to Google's retention practices. Local files and Telegram messages must be removed separately from the systems that hold them.

For a deletion or access request relating to information held in these backups, contact admin@hungrytimes.in. The operator may need to verify the request and consider applicable record-retention obligations.

7. Revoking authorization

The owner can remove this application's access through Google Account connections, disable scheduled jobs and remove saved OAuth tokens from the relevant devices. Revocation stops future authorized access once the revocation takes effect; it does not automatically delete existing backup copies, local data, logs or Telegram messages.

8. These public information pages

These pages have no sign-in form, advertising scripts or analytics scripts of their own. The website's hosting and delivery infrastructure may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, requested URLs and timestamps, to serve and protect the pages.

9. Google API Limited Use

HungryTimes Backups' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

10. Changes and contact

Updates to these practices will be reflected on this page with a revised date. Material changes to the use of Google user data will be disclosed before the changed use and will require renewed consent where applicable.

Questions: admin@hungrytimes.in.
