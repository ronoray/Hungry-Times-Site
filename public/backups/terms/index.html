Terms of use

The operating terms for Hungry Times' private backup and recovery utility.

Effective and last updated: 21 September 2026.

1. Purpose and availability

HungryTimes Backups is an owner-operated utility for backing up, synchronizing, verifying and restoring selected files through Google Drive. Its use is restricted to the owner of Hungry Times and explicitly authorized operators. These public pages describe the utility and do not offer public accounts, paid storage plans or access to stored backups.

2. Authorized accounts and data

An operator must have permission to access the Google account, source systems and data used in a job. Use is limited to legitimate backup and recovery operations. Operators must not use the utility to access another person's account or files without authorization or to bypass a provider's access controls or usage limits.

3. Operator responsibilities

The owner and authorized operators are responsible for selecting source data and destinations, protecting credentials, setting retention periods, reviewing failed-job notifications and confirming that restored data is usable. Database dumps and configuration archives can contain sensitive information and must be handled accordingly.

Verification of an upload or the presence of a recent file does not guarantee that every source file was selected, every job stage completed, or an application can be restored successfully. Restore procedures should be checked separately.

4. Retention and destructive operations

Configured retention jobs may delete older backups. Synchronization and manual maintenance commands can change or remove destination files depending on their settings. Operators are responsible for reviewing the operation and retaining any copies needed for recovery or applicable record-keeping obligations.

5. External services and interruptions

The utility depends on Google Drive, network connectivity, host availability and other configured services. API limits, revoked authorization, outages, unavailable devices and storage problems can interrupt a job or its monitoring. There is no guaranteed completion time, uninterrupted availability or assurance that a notification will always arrive.

Use of external services is also subject to their applicable terms. The utility is independently operated by Hungry Times and is not endorsed by Google, rclone or Telegram.

6. Privacy and ending access

The HungryTimes Backups privacy policy explains data access, storage, notifications, retention and deletion. The owner can stop use by disabling the jobs and revoking the application's access through Google Account connections. Existing backup copies and operational records remain until removed through their respective retention or deletion processes.

7. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the private utility changes. The current version is published here with its effective date. Questions about permitted use or operation should be sent to admin@hungrytimes.in.
